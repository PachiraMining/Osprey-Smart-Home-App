### Description
```
Control your entire smart home from one app. Osprey Home+ lets you manage curtains and other smart IoT devices from anywhere — simple, fast, and secure.

WHY OSPREY HOME+

- Smart Device Control: Open, close, and adjust your smart curtains and other devices with one tap from anywhere.
- Scenes & Automation: Create custom scenes to control multiple devices at once. Set daily, weekly, or one-time schedules to automate your home.
- Tap-to-Run: Execute your favorite scenes instantly from the home screen — no digging through menus.
- Easy Device Setup: Add new devices via Bluetooth with guided step-by-step setup and QR code scanning. Connect to Wi-Fi in seconds.
- Home & Room Organization: Organize devices by rooms across multiple homes for quick access.
- Voice Control: Connect with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant for hands-free control of your devices.
- Secure & Private: All data is encrypted and transmitted securely. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.

PERMISSIONS EXPLAINED

- Bluetooth: Required to discover and set up new smart home devices
- Location: Required by the operating system for Bluetooth scanning — we do not track your location
- Camera: Used only for scanning QR codes during device setup
- Local Network: Required for communicating with devices on your home network
```
